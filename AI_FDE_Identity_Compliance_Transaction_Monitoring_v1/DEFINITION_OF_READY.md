# Definition of Ready
A change is ready for implementation only when all applicable statements are true:
- Business outcome is stated.
- Requirement ID exists.
- Scope and out-of-scope are explicit.
- Acceptance criteria are testable.
- API/data compatibility impact is known.
- Security/privacy implications have been assessed.
- Dependencies and constraints are known.
- Conflicts with brownfield behavior are documented.
- Open policy questions are resolved or captured as an approved assumption/change request.
